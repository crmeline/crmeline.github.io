AniLog

Dec 2025

An anime tracker that makes sure you never miss a new episode

[Overview]

AniLog was made during the Backswitch x UC Workshop, a 3 day program where we designed and prototyped a mobile app from start to finish. It was a guided project to practice clean interfaces, interactions, and basic product flows.

[Problem]

With so many anime titles and weekly releases, it’s hard to remember what you’re watching, where you left off, and when a new episode drops.

[Solution]

A simple anime tracker where you log your progress, see updates, and get to your shows quickly. The design stays clear and low effort, so you can stay organized without feeling overwhelmed.

Design Decisions

[A home screen that’s yours]

A “Hi Jenni” dashboard puts your last watch, recent activity, and new releases in one place.

[Progress in one tap]

You update your episode right from the show page and see how far along you are straight away.

[A quiet update feed]

A clean list tells you when a new episode is out, and the bottom bar keeps Home, Favorites, and Profile easy to reach.

Learnings

This project taught me how to use Figma Auto Layout and add small micro animations that make interactions feel smoother. I also learned how to build a consistent flow from the home screen to the detail pages, and link everything into a prototype with transitions.
